# Charter

Watch Desk helps ship watchstanders understand their schedule without reading hundreds of lines of message text. Its primary experience is a full-width Gantt-style calendar of extracted ship events, with a separate searchable message-records experience for tracing events to their source and saving unsent acknowledgement drafts. The starting scope is a working user-facing application using an existing backend when available, otherwise clearly labeled fictional USS Meridian data. Success means accurate UTC scheduling, readable event labels, reliable source-message navigation, and clear visibility of revisions and incomplete information.
